Our Charism

01

Evangelizing the Poor

Our primary mission is modeled after Christ’s message to bring the Gospel specifically to the poor, abandoned , and marginalized.

02

Mary Immaculate

Mary serves as our spiritual model , patroness , and mother, demonstrating complete openness to the Holy Spirit and God’s will.

03

Total Oblation

The word oblates comes from oblatus meaning a complete offering of oneself to God for the servce of the Church.

04

Life in Community

Oblates live and work closely as a band  of brothers , drawing strength and unity from communal prayer, the Eucharist, and the word of God.

05

Christ and Evangelization

Our charism originates from a story; it is the story of a young man, Eugene de Mazenod. Throughout his life, he experienced in himself the merciful love of God, manifested in Jesus Christ, the Crucified Savior. We too, inspired by our Founder, recognize the power of Christ in us, and we want to  EVANGELIZE, that is to say, to make Him known and to show by our lives the joy of the Good News.

06

Missionaries

We are MISSIONARIES. Once Pope Pius XI referred to us as “Specialists in the difficult missions of the Church”.  We want to dedicate our lives to all those who either do not know Jesus Christ or have forgotten Him in their lives, wherever they are in the world and therefore many Oblates serve in lands outside their own. In mission, we search for our way to holiness.

Origins of oblates in zimbabwe

Men and Women for Others

"Going Forward in the ways of the Lord"— St. Eugene de Mazenod 1782–1861

The OMISA Province of the Oblates of Mary Immaculate was formed in 2017 through the amalgamation of three South African provinces . The province represents a consolidation of Oblate missionary efforts in Southern Africa, ensuring stronger governance , support for vocations , and expansion of ministries in Zimbabwe and beyond.
