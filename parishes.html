 Parishes and Missions in Zimbabwe

The Zimbabwe Mission of the Oblates of Mary Immaculate (OMI), under the OMISA Province, is structured around several parishes and mission stations in Bulawayo and Masvingo dioceses, complemented by special ministries such as youth empowerment, formation houses, and social development projects. These ministries reflect the Oblate charism of serving the poor and most abandoned. 

The Oblates currently serve in the following parishes and missions:

  • 1.Our Lady of Lourdes Parish, Kumalo-

    The first parish entrusted to the Oblates in 1983, located in Bulawayo.

  • 2.Body and Blood of Christ Parish -

    A vibrant parish community in Bulawayo.

  • 3.St Luke’s Mission -

    A rural mission station where the first Zimbabwean-born Oblate priest , FR Chrales Rensburg OMI, was assigned.

  • 4.St. Kizito Mission -

    A newer mission station serving rural commuities.

  • 5.St John Maraire Mission -

    Opened in 2020 in Masvingo  Diocese , expanding Oblate presence beyond Bulawayo.

  • 6.Mazenod  House of Formation -

    Serves as a formation centre for pre-novices and scholastics.

  • 7.Mission House, Bulawayo-

    Adminstrative centre for the Zimbabwe Mission.

Special Ministries in Zimbabwe

Beyond parish work, the Oblates engage in special ministries that embody their missionary spirit:

• Youth Ministry– A youth empowerment initiative launched in the 2000s, focusing on faith formation, leadership, and lifeskills.

• Formation House – The pre-novitiate at Mazenod House, dedicated to nurturing local vocations.

• Social Development Projects – Borehole drilling, community gardens, and counselling services to uplift rural communities.

• Education and Training – Collaboration with other religious congregations to train new formators and support Catholic education.

• Pastoral Outreach – Expanding missionary presence in rural areas like St. Kizito and Maraire, despite economic challenges.
